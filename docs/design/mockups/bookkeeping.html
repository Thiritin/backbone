How the mockup works

This is the only billing app at game start. Everything in it is done by hand, and every greyed button is a Pear Store feature that takes one of those hand steps away.

Customers

An accepted order lands here as a new entry with the order email beside an empty form. Click a line in the email to put it into the focused field. Save creates the customer and a draft invoice. The product and the server stay in Locations, so this screen only holds who pays and where the invoice goes.

Later: the shop order form fills this in itself.

Invoices

Every customer gets a draft each month. Open it, check the lines, click Send as PDF. The invoice goes out by email and is due in 14 days.

Later: Monthly run sends every draft at once.

Bank

Payments arrive as transfers. Pick a transfer, then the invoice it pays. Amounts that differ or references that are missing have to be judged by the player.

Later: Match by reference books the clean ones itself.

Reminders

Invoices past due appear here. Each click sends the next reminder level, and the third level suspends the services.

Later: Automatic reminders run the levels on schedule.
