# Documents and archive

Built: DocuWhere, a Pear Store app that files the paper post.

## Post

Every letter lands in the Post app, which the first letter puts on the dock. Post stays a flat timeline: newest on top, no folders, no search. That is the free baseline.

## DocuWhere

**DocuWhere** (a pun on DocuWare) is a Pear Store app on the Essentials shelf for €5 a month in 2005, with no skill needed. Once installed it shows every arrived letter in a window laid out like an archive client:

- A side panel with the archive (all documents, and the inbox of unread letters) and the document types with a count each.
- A search field above the hit list. Search matches the sender, the subject and the letter text.
- The hit list with date, sender, subject and document type, newest first.
- A preview of the selected letter, the same page the Post app shows, with the document type above it.

The document types are contract, invoice, official letter, registration and other. A letter gets its type when it arrives: a letter with a bill is an invoice, a letter from the trade office or the commercial register is a registration, a letter from the tax office, the notary, the chamber or the DohleFormular service is an official letter, and anything else is other. The player can file any letter under another type in the preview, and that choice is kept on the letter.

The German labels follow real archive software: Ablage, Posteingang, Dokumenttyp, Suche.

## Open

- Desktop forms and certificates are not in the archive yet; they have no sender or arrival date.
- No sim event sends a contract by post yet, so contract is only ever picked by the player.
- Paying a bill or opening a letter's link still happens in the Post app.
