**Support desks.** A hosted desk is subscribed to on its vendor's site at a per-seat price: the monthly price is charged once for the owner plus once for every employee with the support role (bots do not count), with the yearly rise of 8%, at sign-up and at every month start. A legacy desk from a save that bought it in the Pear Store is billed the same way. The self-hosted desks cost nothing. Each desk is available from its year. Anchors: osTicket has been free and open source since before 2005, Zendesk launched in 2007 at about USD 49 a seat, Freshdesk launched in June 2011 at about USD 15 a seat, FreeScout came out in November 2018 and is free. Every feature starts on a desk when the real product got it: in a calendar year for the hosted desks, which the vendor upgrades for every tenant at once, and with the release that introduced it for the self-hosted ones. The dates and their sources are in software-versions.md, Support desks.

AI on the hosted desks starts with the real features: suggested articles from 2013 on Zendusk and 2011 on Freshdusk, the answer bot from 2017 on Zendusk (Zendesk Answer Bot, about USD 1 a resolution) and 2018 on Freshdusk (Freddy), and generative auto-answer and reply drafting from 2023 on both. Suggested articles are on from the start and only on hosted desks: of the customers who would open a basic how-to ticket on the portal form (30%), one in five reads the suggested article instead, on top of the Knowledge base item. The answer bot is switched on in the desk's AI page and replies once to every new basic how-to ticket with the topic's article; one in four customers marks it solved, which costs €1 and is billed as one "Answer bot" ledger line at the month start, and the rest wait for an agent with their original due time and no penalty. Generative auto-answer and reply drafting need the Automation skill and cost a flat €50 add-on a month while either is on, plus their tokens (see Desk AI usage in staff-salaries-skill-growth-and-quitting.md); auto-answer replaces the answer bot while it is on. A cancellation from a customer paying at least €50 a month is high priority in the desk, an outage is high and urgent for a high-budget customer, appeals and takedown deadlines are high, how-to questions are low and everything else is normal.

A desk with a customer portal takes 30% of the outage, how-to and billing requests through its web form while it is reachable, and those need no mailbox; the rest arrive by email as before. While the desk is down every customer writes by email.

Installing pays the first month at once and is refused without the cash for it; later months are billed on the 1st as "Pear One" in the ledger. Cancelling stops billing and keeps the skill. Saves from before the Pear Store keep every widget, billed like a new purchase. The per-device, per-customer and per-employee extras and the surprise price adjustments are not in the game yet.

The monitoring and virtualization products' own licences (see the software choices) come on top of the Pear module.

**In-app purchases.** One-off extras inside an expansion, priced per use: an instant invoice run outside the monthly cycle (€2), an alert text message (€0.10 each), an extra dashboard slot (€3 per month), a priority remote-hands booking (€25).
