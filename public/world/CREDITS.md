# World art credits

The room's sprites are cropped from the pack below into `atlas.png` by `scripts/world-atlas.mjs`; the pack's sheets are not part of the repository.

| Files | Pack | Author | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `atlas.png` | Modern Data Center Top-Down Pixel Art Tileset | Cute SCKR | itch.io | Commercial use in games allowed, no standalone redistribution |
| `src/ui/map/roads.ts` | Natural Earth 10m roads | Natural Earth | https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/roads/ | Public domain |
