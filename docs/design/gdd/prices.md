# Prices

Starting values come from today's Western European market (checked October 2026, excluding VAT, dollars converted at about €0.86). Because the game starts around 2005, transit starts higher and falls every year until it reaches these levels, while hardware prices follow the parts, whose prices change by year (economy/README.md, Part prices by year).
